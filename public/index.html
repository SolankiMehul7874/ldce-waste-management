How Campus Circular Recovery Works

A closed-loop 3-step operational methodology

1

Source Segregation at Corridors

Modular 4-stream stations with mechanical hands-free foot pedals and trilingual pictograms prevent waste commingling at the point of discard.

2

In-Situ Composting & Recycling

Wet canteen scraps are turned into nutrient-rich organic compost with chaas starter; dry recyclables are routed to authorized AMC scrap processors.

3

Digital Audit Telemetry

Corridor QR codes allow daily gravimetric logging, automatic diversion recalculation, and fill-level telemetry reporting.
